## How to activate

**Option 1 — Dashboard (recommended):**

1. Go to [ayteeel.cfd/dashboard](https://ayteeel.cfd/dashboard) and sign in with Discord.
2. Select the server you want Premium on.
3. Choose **Get Monthly** or **Get Yearly** and complete payment.
4. The purchase is applied to the selected server **automatically**.

**Option 2 — Redeem code:**

```
/redeem code:<your-code>
```

Codes are issued on purchase or by the bot owner.

**If your purchase doesn't appear:** join the [support server](https://discord.gg/EEHuxXM97H) and contact the bot owner with your proof of purchase.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I cancel my monthly subscription?**
Yes. Monthly Premium can be cancelled before the next billing period. When you purchase Premium you receive an email from our payment partner — cancel from there.

**What happens when Premium expires?**
Premium-only features are disabled, while your existing server data remains intact. Re-subscribing re-enables them.

**Can I use Premium on multiple servers?**
No. A subscription covers one server. To move it to another server, contact the bot owner in the [support server](https://discord.gg/EEHuxXM97H).

**How do I activate my purchase?**
Purchases are applied automatically to the server you selected at checkout. If it doesn't show up, contact the bot owner on [Discord](https://discord.gg/EEHuxXM97H).

**Do I get a refund?**
Contact support before the next billing period; see [terms](https://ayteeel.cfd/terms) for full details.
